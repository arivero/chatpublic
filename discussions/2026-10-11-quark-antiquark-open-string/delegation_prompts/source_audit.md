## Reusable patterns supported by the published material

Patterns 4–6 below are **observations in the published reasoning synopses**, not instructions quoted from original prompts.

1. **Give one precise research target and allow disproof.** The [spin-glass prompt, pp. 1–2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/mezard-parisi-formula.pdf#page=1) asks for a “complete rigorous solution” or “rigorous refutation,” then states the exact limiting equality, disorder assumptions, trial functional, and order of quantifiers. The [Heisenberg prompt, p. 1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/spontaneous-magnetization-quantum-heisenberg-ferromagnet.pdf#page=1) states a prove/refute target with a specified counterexample standard. Adaptation: each research subagent should receive one falsifiable claim and an explicit acceptable negative outcome.

2. **Define the object and boundary conditions before asking for a mechanism.** The [Heisenberg prompt, p. 1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/spontaneous-magnetization-quantum-heisenberg-ferromagnet.pdf#page=1) fixes lattice, spin representation, Hamiltonian, KMS definition, and the exact magnetization claim; it excludes a rotation-invariant mixture as the target. The [Vlasov–Maxwell prompt, pp. 1–2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/relativistic-vlasov-maxwell.pdf#page=1) fixes normalization, data regularity, constraints, and what a negative resolution entails. Adaptation: state our retained-adjoint field content, endpoint assignment, contact interaction, units, and observable before asking an agent for a calculation.

3. **State which assumptions are premises and which are open obligations.** The [Mahler summary, p. 1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/symmetric-and-general-mahler-conjectures.pdf#page=1) expressly separates its three attempts and says Part II receives a bound as a supplied premise, without identifying that premise with Part I’s candidate proof. The [spin-glass prompt, p. 2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/mezard-parisi-formula.pdf#page=2) makes clear that its formula takes an infimum over all finite levels and asserts no finite-level attainment. Adaptation: mark inherited derivations “assumed for this branch” and assign a separate reviewer if that premise matters.

4. **Ask for a concrete failure test of the candidate argument.** The [Heisenberg synopsis, pp. 3–4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/spontaneous-magnetization-quantum-heisenberg-ferromagnet.pdf#page=3) revisits conditioning, sign, mass-vs-pointwise control, and a possible overstrong negative-dependence claim. The [Vlasov–Maxwell synopsis, pp. 3–5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/relativistic-vlasov-maxwell.pdf#page=3) finds a too-long stability interval, stronger Sobolev requirements in a cited continuation criterion, and unresolved cutoff terms. Adaptation: a checking subagent should seek the first counterexample, hidden regularity assumption, dimensional inconsistency, or unjustified limit, with the exact step identified.

5. **Maintain a dependency ledger and report conditional conclusions.** The [Vlasov–Maxwell synopsis, p. 5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/relativistic-vlasov-maxwell.pdf#page=5) ends by saying the asserted continuation rests on occupation, direction-count, and cutoff estimates; a cited continuation theorem alone does not prove them. The [spin-glass synopsis, pp. 3–4](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/mezard-parisi-formula.pdf#page=3) tracks a needed averaged error, then the order of limits and truncation removal. Adaptation: require every agent to label proven, computed, assumed, and unresolved links; a conditional radius estimate stays conditional.

6. **Use small, discriminating checks before expanding.** The [spin-glass synopsis, p. 3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/mezard-parisi-formula.pdf#page=3) first probes edge-case assumptions and uses a constant-interaction normalization check. The [Mahler synopsis, p. 2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/reasoning_traces/symmetric-and-general-mahler-conjectures.pdf#page=2) checks normalization, genericity, boundary ties, and extremal examples. Adaptation: for our model, use a limiting coupling, symmetry, or units check that can actually falsify the proposed step; avoid routine computation performed just to produce check counts.

## Limits of the evidence

No result inspected in this audit has yet supplied a direct mathematical or physical ingredient for the retained-adjoint model. The user raised this distinction explicitly: methodological examples are not evidence for our theory, and topical proximity of BFSS does not establish applicability. This audit therefore does not change the active research priority or justify a broader search without a concrete connection.

The repository includes mathematical physics results, including [BFSS](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/CONTENTS.md#L6797-L6815) and [quantum field theory](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/CONTENTS.md#L7059-L7061), but its ten released reasoning summaries do not include a string-endpoint or quark-model prompt. I did not inspect all 719 manuscripts or prove absence of prompts hidden inside every PDF.
