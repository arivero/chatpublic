# Bounded unconventional candidate search

**Model role:** inherit the parent research model.

**Inputs:** [idea bank](../../blackboard/ideas.md), [workplan](../../notes/workplan.md), `{{BASE_COMMIT}}`; parent names one open `{{BOTTLENECK}}`. Write only `{{OUTPUT_PATH}}`.

**Task:** Invent at most three distinct mechanisms for `{{BOTTLENECK}}` while retaining the retained-adjoint model as the primary concrete object and the four-sector string goal. For each, supply minimal fields/action or operator, the mechanism's causal chain, one discriminating calculable test, and its principal objection plus revival condition. Spend most effort developing the strongest one to a first nontrivial equation. Evaluate exact charges and statistics without rejecting ideas by labels or repeating old objections without new analysis.

**Deliverable and stop:** At most three candidate cards and one worked diagnostic in `{{OUTPUT_PATH}}`; stop before declaring a full theory.

**Pitfall:** Avoid agreement bias and familiar-model substitution; a candidate can be useful even when its current evidence is conditional or negative.
