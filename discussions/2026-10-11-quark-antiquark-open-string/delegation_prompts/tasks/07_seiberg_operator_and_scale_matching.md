**Inputs:** [Seiberg notes](../../blackboard/005_fermionic_binding_and_seiberg.md), [workplan](../../notes/workplan.md), `{{BASE_COMMIT}}`; parent chooses `{{ELECTRIC_MAGNETIC_PAIR}}`. Write only `{{OUTPUT_PATH}}`.

**Task:** For the specified electric/magnetic pair, give a literal endpoint/operator map for one fermionic string candidate and its bosonic comparison sector. Use gauge-invariant operators for singlets and a stated dressing or gauge prescription for colored interpolators. Track parity, B/L and flavor charges, anomalies, superpotential, light modes, matching scales, and the energy window where magnetic variables are weak. Identify which ingredients could reproduce retained-adjoint binding and which are only analogies. State effects of any supersymmetry-breaking or mass deformation on rank and phase without extrapolating beyond control.

**Deliverable and stop:** One operator and scale dictionary with one calculable observable or a sharply identified mismatch in `{{OUTPUT_PATH}}`. Stop before claiming four-sector completion.

**Pitfall:** An ordinary scalar–fermion mesino or symmetry-allowed overlap is not automatically the intended string or a computed constituent probability.
