The article proposes treating the neutrino as a fermionic Goldstone particle and constructs invariant neutrino self-interactions (Eqs. 6–8). It also gives an invariant action for a generic Dirac field (Eq. 9) and sketches weak gauge fields for the neutrino and other leptons. It does **not** discuss a pion, muon, meson superpartner, charged-lepton generation count, or pion decay to a muon plus a Goldstino. Those are separate proposed interpretations and must not be attributed to this source.
