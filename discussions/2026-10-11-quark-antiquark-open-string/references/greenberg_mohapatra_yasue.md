# Greenberg–Mohapatra–Yasue: relevance to the current model

Read 13 October 2026. The user-supplied four-page original was read in full with Poppler native text extraction; consequential equations and qualifications were checked on rendered original pp. 1738–1739. The other two sources below were checked through publisher-sourced abstracts in INSPIRE and Crossref metadata only; their full texts were not available in this search. No result below is a validation of the theories.

## Original supplied paper: the actual result

**O. W. Greenberg, R. N. Mohapatra and M. Yasue**, [“Determination of the Number of Generations of Quarks and Leptons from Flavor-Color Symmetry,”](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.51.1737) *Physical Review Letters* **51** (7 November 1983), 1737–1740; [archived PDF](received-2026-10-13/physrevlett.51.1737.pdf), [native extraction](received-2026-10-13/physrevlett.51.1737.txt).

This is a **supersymmetric preon/hypercolor** construction, not a QCD meson–lepton partner assignment. Two chiral preon superfields transform under confining `SU(N)_H`. Each has six flavor–color labels, partitioned as two weak-flavor labels **plus** four `SU(4)` color/lepton labels (three quark colors plus one lepton label): `6=2+4` in their global `SU(6)`, not a product count. Assumed symmetry breaking leaves `H = SU(4)_L × SU(2)_L × SU(4)_R × SU(2)_R` [Eq. (4), p. 1738]. Composite quarks and leptons arise as quasi-Nambu–Goldstone fermions, using supersymmetry and chiral symmetry to keep them light. The paper assumes `N=N_fc=6` from an allowed condensate [Eq. (3)] and uses an `SU(4)` anomaly-matching index equation `2(l_1−l_2+l_3)=6` [Eq. (5)]. Choosing `l_1=3`, the other indices zero [Eq. (6)], gives three light generations. Its compact statement is `2N_g=N=N_fc` [Eq. (8), p. 1739]. Thus **three is conditional** on the preon content, the assumed condensates and unbroken group, the choice of no other massless charged fermions, and the index solution; it is not a model-independent theorem about three generations.

The paper is directly useful as a *mechanism to test*: protect composite fermions through supersymmetric Goldstone structure, then impose the unbroken symmetry, anomaly matching and absence of unwanted light states. It distinguishes one doubly protected generation from two singly protected generations (p. 1738). Its illustrative intergeneration mass matrix [Eq. (7), original p. 1738] depends on the supersymmetry-breaking scale `M_s`, an `SU(4)_L×SU(4)_R` breaking scale `m_4`, and hypercolor scale `Λ_H`; p. 1739 calls the resulting masses only “in qualitative agreement” and explicitly says “To discuss the weak mixing pattern needs further study.” Eq. (7) concerns **fermion generation mixing**, not a six-charged-meson flavor mixing matrix. It does not provide physical π/K/D/Ds/B/Bc partners or a specific meson–lepton map.

The authors explicitly identify `U(1)_X` as an **R symmetry** on p. 1738, and introduce `U(1)_V` preon number and `U(1)_A` in Eqs. (1)–(3). The original p. 1738 gives axial charges `Q_A(A^(s))=N/6−1` for the scalar preon, `Q_A(ψ^(s))=N/6` for its fermion, and `Q_A(λ)=−1` for the gaugino. At the chosen `N=6` these are respectively `0,+1,−1`. This is relevant R-symmetry provenance, but the paper does **not** calculate the `B`, `L`, `B−L` or R charge of a supercharge mapping a physical QCD meson to a lepton, nor resolve endpoint spin assignments in this project's four-string model. Its quark/lepton `SU(4)` label is a preon representation label. The paper also does not establish that composite leptons meet observed pointlike form-factor limits; a full dynamics and scale-dependent current calculation would be needed to transfer its idea to this project.

## Two direct precursor sources cited in the PRL

- **O. W. Greenberg, R. N. Mohapatra and M. Yasue**, [“Weak interaction symmetry and generation structure in the quasi-Nambu-Goldstone fermion picture of quarks and leptons,”](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(83)90074-6) *Physics Letters B* **128** (1983), 65–68. [INSPIRE 13692](https://inspirehep.net/literature/13692) returns the publisher-sourced abstract: massless composite quarks and leptons as superpartners of composite Goldstone bosons from broken global symmetry with non-Abelian hypercolor confinement, and different generation schemes. This confirms the broad mechanism predates the November PRL. **Abstract only** was read; no specific construction or formula from its full text is claimed here.
- **W. Buchmüller, R. D. Peccei and T. Yanagida**, [“Quarks and leptons as quasi Nambu-Goldstone fermions,”](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(83)91405-3) *Physics Letters B* **124** (1983), 67–73. [INSPIRE 181736](https://inspirehep.net/literature/181736) returns the publisher-sourced abstract. It proposes light composite quarks/leptons as fermionic partners of Goldstone bosons from broken global symmetry in a supersymmetric preon theory. Its abstract says `SU(5)→SU(3)×U(1)_em` produces one family and mentions a supersymmetric nonlinear effective Lagrangian and unresolved family/mass questions. The uploaded PRL credits this as its mass-protection mechanism (p. 1737 and reference 6). **Abstract only** was read; the effective Lagrangian was not inspected.

The relationship to Rivero's 2005–2009 `sBootstrap` is limited but potentially fruitful: both place composite bosons and light fermions in a supersymmetry narrative, while the **composite object and direction of the mapping differ**. Greenberg–Mohapatra–Yasue have composite Goldstone bosons and **composite quark/lepton fermions** in a preon theory; Rivero proposes conventional QCD mesons as scalar partners of **elementary quarks/leptons**. Their symmetry-breaking/anomaly audit can guide a new candidate, but their `2N_g=N_fc` result cannot be inserted into Rivero's five-flavor charged-meson count, and their mass matrix does not supply the six-state pairing or pointlike-string mechanism.

## Separate modern SQCD bridge to evaluate

**Nathan Seiberg**, [“Exact Results on the Space of Vacua of Four Dimensional SUSY Gauge Theories,” arXiv:hep-th/9402044v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9402044v1), submitted 8 February 1994, §4, original printed p. 7 (PDF p. 8), Eq. (4.1). The primary PDF and relevant passage were read. For supersymmetric `SU(N_c)` gauge theory with `N_f=N_c`, the **quantum** moduli constraint is `det M−B\tilde B=Λ^{2N_c}`, modifying the classical `det M−B\tilde B=0` [§2, Eq. (2.8)]. Seiberg says the change comes from a one-instanton effect and removes the classical singular origin. This exact constraint could inform a modern composite-meson/baryon operator audit, but applies to that particular supersymmetric gauge theory. It does not by itself supply a Greenberg–Mohapatra–Yasue vacuum, a pion–lepton supercharge, or a physical retained-adjoint/string spectrum. No direct phase equivalence is asserted here.
