 Pointlike Scattering from Fermionic Open Strings and a Bootstrap Interpretation of
                                      Quarks

                A finite open string can scatter, at tree level, more pointlike than a generic composite fermion. For a
              charged Ramond state we show that the Møller-like amplitude is the massless gauge-theory amplitude
              multiplied by a Veneziano factor. The first string correction is O(α′2 E 4 ), with no dimension-six
              contact term in the same-brane sector. We derive the angular signal and discuss the limited but
              concrete sense in which this realizes a quark–diquark bootstrap.
