# Focused source reading, 13 October 2026

Scope: original Volkov–Akulov upload and targeted search for **explicit three-generation meson–lepton supersymmetry**. Primary arXiv PDFs were downloaded and native text extracted for reading; the original upload was also viewed as page images. This is a record of what these papers say, not confirmation of their models.

## The supplied 1973 source

- **D. V. Volkov and V. P. Akulov**, [“Is the Neutrino a Goldstone Particle?”](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(73)90490-5), *Physics Letters B* **46** (1973), 109–110; [archived original](received-2026-10-13/volkov_akulov_1973.pdf). The two-page original was read. The hypothesis is that a neutrino is a fermionic Goldstone particle. Equations (6)–(8) construct its self-interaction; Eq. (9) couples the nonlinear field to a generic Dirac field. The closing paragraphs discuss other leptons and weak gauge interactions. The paper contains **no pion, muon, charged-lepton/meson superpartner assignment, generation count, or interpretation of π → μν as π → μ + Goldstino**. Any such link is a separate conjecture.

## Direct prior art found

- **Alejandro Rivero**, [“Supersymmetry with composite bosons,” arXiv:hep-ph/0512065v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0512065v1), submitted 5 December 2005. The primary four-page PDF was read. Its abstract explicitly proposes extending hadronic quark–diquark supersymmetry into the lepton sector and says the three-generation degree count can build charged supermultiplets. PDF p. 1 says the mesons “provide us 6 charged +1 degrees of freedom, ready to fit with the charged leptons” and that the neutral assignment is model dependent. PDF p. 3, Table 2 lists charged quark–antiquark flavor pairs from five meson-forming quarks, including π-, K-, D-, Ds-, B-, and Bc-like content. Its p. 1 arithmetic assumes the top does not form a meson: with three down-type and two up-type flavors, `D U = 2N` and `D(D+1)/2 = 2N` give `N=3`. The paper itself leaves extra charge-4/3 diquarks and neutral-state assignment unresolved, and gives no complete dynamical model or unique mapping of its six mesons into three named lepton pairs. (The PDF carries a later internal typesetting date, 9 November 2018; the arXiv v1 deposit date establishes the source date.)
- **Alejandro Rivero**, [“Third Spectroscopy with a hint of superstrings,” arXiv:0710.1526v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.1526v2), first submitted October 2007. The primary PDF was read, especially §3, pp. 3–4. It explicitly proposes elementary fermions and composite QCD open strings in common supermultiplets. Its oriented-string meson count `2n=rs`, `4n=r²+s²−1`, plus a diquark count, selects the minimal `n=3`, `r=3` down-type, `s=2` up-type flavor configuration. It expressly flags extra charge-4/3 diquarks and mass/chirality mechanisms as open problems. This is a counting argument conditional on its assumptions, not a realized supercharge.
- **Alejandro Rivero**, [“Unbroken supersymmetry, without new particles,” arXiv:0910.4793v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.4793v2), revised 12 November 2009. The primary PDF was read, especially §II, PDF pp. 2–3, §IV, p. 6, and §V, pp. 6–7. The `sBootstrap` discussion explicitly points to “the masses of muon and tau” as near “the pion and the D meson” (p. 2), takes `u,c,d,s,b` as five meson-forming flavors with top excluded, and decomposes their SU(5) meson and diquark representations. Section IV names π, K, B, D, Ds, Bc as six charged pseudoscalar flavor states to be mixed. PDF p. 3 calls the undetected string structure in electron spin and charge “a major objection against the sBootstrap mechanism”; the paper does not resolve it. Its neutral lepton and chirality assignments are also unsettled.
- **Rivero**, [guest post “sBootstrap”](https://dorigo.wordpress.com/2007/10/16/guest-post-alejandro-rivero-sbootstrap/), *A Quantum Diaries Survivor*, 16 October 2007. The post text was retrieved. It states the same three-generation `r=3,s=2` count and expressly identifies top's inability to form mesons as an assumption. It points to the arXiv:0710.1526 paper. The article is author-written public exposition; the arXiv PDFs above are the closer technical sources.

These sources establish that the broad three-generation composite-meson/charged-lepton SUSY proposal **has explicit prior art**. The particular example pairing `μ ↔ (π,K)`, `τ ↔ (D,Ds)`, `e ↔ (B,Bc)` was **not located as an asserted pairing in the read sources**. The six charged flavor states and three charged Dirac leptons are counted, but assignment after flavor mixing is not fixed by that count. A resemblance of masses does not establish supersymmetry or the specific pair assignment. The cited papers do not derive a supersymmetry transformation mapping physical pions to muons, nor a contact-bound retained-adjoint construction for this project's fermionic strings.

Targeted searches of these three Rivero PDFs found no treatment of a supercharge's baryon number, lepton number, `B−L`, `R` charge/parity, or endpoint spin assignment. The 2007 paper (§3, PDF p. 3) asks to match spin-0 composite bosons to spin-1/2 fermions, and the 2009 paper (PDF p. 4) imagines sfermions as quarks at string ends; neither passage supplies the charge ledger or resolves a spin ambiguity. Thus those specific proposed checks should be credited to the present analysis, while the broad meson–lepton idea and three-generation count should cite Rivero.
