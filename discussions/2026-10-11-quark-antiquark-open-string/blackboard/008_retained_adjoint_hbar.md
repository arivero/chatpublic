# 008 — Retained adjoint with explicit physical units

**Question.** Can a retained adjoint fermion bind both fermionic endpoints through contact interactions, with no direct endpoint force, and what do recoil and the whole-state current imply? This is the user's selected concrete object within the broader four-string program. Full assumptions and derivations are in [paper version 0.3](../manuscript/retained_adjoint_hbar.tex).

**Model and units.** Fixed particle number, global color, two endpoint–adjoint regulated Gaussian contacts of range $R$ and strength $G$; no direct endpoint interaction. Keep $\hbar$ explicit: $[G]=$ energy × length³ and $\mathbf p=-i\hbar\nabla$. Relativistic control refers to momenta compared with $mc_{\rm light}$ and $Mc_{\rm light}$. Physical transfer $Q$ has wave number $Q/\hbar$ in a current matrix element.

**Conditional results.** The paper's sufficient dimer condition is $G>(3\sqrt3\pi^{3/2}/4)\hbar^2R/\mu$. It implies $E_3\leq2\epsilon_2<\epsilon_2$, below the dimer-plus-free-endpoint threshold. With $\eta=\mu/M$, recoil has zero first-order energy shift and a negative order-$\eta^2$ shift. For a deep Gaussian well, $\varepsilon=\hbar/(\mu\omega R^2)\ll1$, and

$$E_3=-2V_0+\frac{3\hbar\omega}{2}S_\eta-\frac{15\hbar^2}{32\mu R^2}S_\eta^2+O(V_0\varepsilon^3).$$

The harmonic current has a correlated nonzero mean-square charge radius when total charge is nonzero; its form factor resolves length $\hbar/Q$. Binding alone therefore does not prove pointlike scattering. The contact Born series and the semiclassical $\hbar$ expansion have different controls.
