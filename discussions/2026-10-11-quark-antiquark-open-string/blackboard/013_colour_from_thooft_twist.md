## 1. Question

Blackboard 012 ended with gauge groups U(15) or U(6) × U(9), in which the product structure 15 = 5 × 3 is a choice of basis. Can a flux background of the ten-dimensional gauge field turn that structure into physics, with SU(3) colour as the unbroken group and the five-fold index as something else?

## 2. Setting

A U(N) bundle on a two-torus with sides L₁, L₂ and first Chern number m has transition functions that can be taken constant on adjoint fields,

    Φ(x₁ + L₁, x₂) = Ω₁ Φ Ω₁⁻¹,    Φ(x₁, x₂ + L₂) = Ω₂ Φ Ω₂⁻¹,    Ω₁Ω₂ = e^{2πi m/N} Ω₂Ω₁.

With g = gcd(m, N), N = g n and m = g m′, a solution is Ω₁ = U ⊗ 1_g, Ω₂ = V^{m′} ⊗ 1_g, where U and V are the n × n clock and shift matrices, UV = e^{2πi/n} VU. The label space factorizes as C^N = C^n ⊗ C^g. In brane language this is a stack of g branes wrapped n times with m′ units of flux.

## 3. Results

### T1. A twist with flux 3, 6, 9 or 12 leaves exactly U(3)

For N = 15 the unbroken group is the commutant of the twist, U(gcd(m,15)). Flux m = 3m′ with m′ = 1, 2, 3, 4 gives C^15 = C^5 ⊗ C^3 with unbroken group 1₅ ⊗ U(3). The three-fold index is colour because it is the index the twist leaves alone. The five-fold index carries the irreducible representation of the pair (U, V): it is a wrapping number.

### T2. The meson matrix becomes a U(3) field on a noncommutative torus with θ = s/5

Expand an adjoint field as Φ = Σ_{(a,b) ∈ Z₅²} U^a V^b ⊗ φ_{ab}(x), with each φ_{ab} a 3 × 3 matrix. The twist gives φ_{ab} momenta in (2π/L₁)(Z + b/5) and (2π/L₂)(Z − m′a/5). The single-valued combinations

    Z₁ = V e^{2πi x₁/(5L₁)},    Z₂ = U^{−s} e^{2πi x₂/(5L₂)},    s m′ = 1 mod 5,

generate all of them and satisfy Z₁Z₂ = e^{2πi s/5} Z₂Z₁. So the algebra of meson-like fields, which the sBootstrap reads as (24 ⊕ 1) ⊗ (8 ⊕ 1), is the algebra of 3 × 3 matrices over the rational noncommutative torus with θ = s/5: θ = 1/5, 3/5, 2/5, 4/5 for m′ = 1, 2, 3, 4. This is the rational case of the equivalence introduced by Connes, Douglas and Schwarz. In that description the number of colours is the rank of the gauge group and the number of flavours is the denominator of θ.

### T3. Flavour symmetry is reduced to Z₅ × Z₅ and the 24 become massive

The class (a,b) is conserved modulo 5, and the continuous SU(5) is gone. Only the class (0,0), the U(3) gauge multiplet, has a zero mode. The lowest state in class (a,b) has

    M = (2πħ/c) · sqrt( (b̃/5L₁)² + (ã/5L₂)² ),

with ã, b̃ the representatives of −m′a and b in {−2, …, 2}. On a square torus the 24 non-singlet classes split as 4, 4, 4, 8, 4 at M² proportional to 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, for every m′. Each class carries a full U(3) adjoint, so the colour-octet partners come along as in 012.

### T4. Diquark multiplicities become index numbers

The diquark-like fields are sections of Λ²(E), and Λ²(C⁵ ⊗ C³) = S²(C⁵) ⊗ Λ²(C³) ⊕ Λ²(C⁵) ⊗ S²(C³). With E₅ the rank-5 twisted bundle of Chern number m′, the number of zero modes on the torus is

    colour antitriplets:  c₁(S²E₅) = 6 m′,      colour sextets:  c₁(Λ²E₅) = 4 m′.

The flavour multiplicities 15 and 10 are replaced by 6 and 4 per unit of flux. For general wrapping number n they are (n+1)m′ and (n−1)m′.

### T5. One colour group and a flavour-dependent charge exclude each other

Let S be the algebra generated by the flavour parts of any background of the form f ⊗ 1₃: twists, Wilson lines, or adjoint expectation values. The unbroken group is the unitary group of S′ ⊗ M₃, with S′ the commutant of S in the 5 × 5 matrices. A charge Q = q ⊗ 1₃ is unbroken exactly when q lies in S′.

- A single colour group needs S′ = C, and then q is a multiple of the identity. The twist of T1 is this case: the charge generator with entries 2/3 and −1/3 is broken.
- A charge with two eigenvalues needs S′ to contain C ⊕ C, and then the unbroken group contains U(3) × U(3): one colour group for the (u,c) labels and one for the (d,s,b) labels.

Breaking U(3) × U(3) to the diagonal needs a field charged under both colour groups and neutral under Q. The adjoint of SO(32) has none: its bifundamental components carry charge ±1 or ±1/3.

### T6. The charge-preserving version: two stacks with wrapping numbers 2 and 3

Take E = E_u ⊕ E_d with E_u = C³ ⊗ E₂ (flux m_u odd) and E_d = C³ ⊗ E₃ (flux m_d not divisible by 3), and a line bundle for the sixteenth label.

- The unbroken group is U(3)_u × U(3)_d × U(1) and the charge is Q = (2/3)Q_u − (1/3)Q_d + q_n Q_n, the usual combination of stack charges.
- The label count is 3 × 2 + 3 × 3 + 1 = 16, which is the form the type I tadpole condition takes for wrapped stacks.
- The (u,c) flavour algebra becomes the noncommutative torus with θ = 1/2, whose generators anticommute, and the (d,s,b) algebra the one with θ = 1/3 or 2/3. The three non-singlet (u,c) classes split 2 + 1 and the eight (d,s,b) classes 4 + 4 on a square torus.
- Zero modes on the flux torus:

  | m_u, m_d | uu: 3bar, 6 | dd: 3bar, 6 | ud diquarks | u dbar mesons |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1, 1 | 3, 1 | 4, 2 | 5 | 1 |
  | 1, 2 | 3, 1 | 8, 4 | 7 | 1 |
  | 1, −1 | 3, 1 | 4, 2 | 1 | 5 |
  | 1, −2 | 3, 1 | 8, 4 | 1 | 7 |

  General formulas: uu 3|m_u| and |m_u|; dd 4|m_d| and 2|m_d|; ud |3m_u + 2m_d|; u dbar |3m_u − 2m_d|.
- The charge-4/3 sector comes out as in the paper: three antitriplets and one sextet, the dimensions of S²(2) and Λ²(2). The other two sectors do not: the counts 6 and 6 would need m_d = 3/2.

### T7. A bounded scan on T⁶ finds no better assignment

For factorized fluxes on three two-tori, with stacks u (N = 3, wrapping (2,1,1)), d (N = 3, wrapping (3,1,1) or (1,3,1)) and a neutral brane, and all flux numbers between −4 and 4:

- 161 280 assignments have non-zero uu, ud and dd chiral indices. The indices are 12 Π m_u, Π (n_u m_d + m_u n_d) and 16 Π m_d.
- None has the ratio 1 : 2 : 2 of the paper's counts 3, 6, 6.
- None satisfies the three tadpole conditions Σ N n^i m^j m^k = 0 for any neutral-brane flux in the same range.

This is a statement about the scanned range and the factorized ansatz.

## 4. What this means for the sBootstrap

1. The flux mechanism does what open point 1 of 012 asked: it makes 15 = 5 × 3 physical, with colour as the unbroken group.
2. It does so by turning flavour into wrapping. The multiplicities that the paper counts as dimensions of flavour representations (15 = 3 + 6 + 6, and 24) become index numbers and momentum classes, and they come out different: 6 antitriplets for a five-fold wrapping, or 3, 5 or 7, 4 or 8 in the two-stack version.
3. Electric charge forces the two-stack version, which has two colour groups. This obstruction (T5) holds for every background valued in flavour directions, so it also covers Wilson lines and Higgsing by the meson-like scalars.
4. The paper's counts are reproduced when the flux is absent, which is the situation of 012: flavour as a symmetry, colour not singled out.

So the two requirements pull apart: singling out colour by a background destroys the flavour counting, and keeping the flavour counting leaves colour unseparated.

## 5. Open points

- A background outside the flavour directions, or a mechanism other than symmetry breaking (for instance colour as the only confining factor), is needed to evade T5.
- Flux on one torus breaks supersymmetry and has tachyonic directions in the charged sectors; stable configurations need flux on several tori, more stacks or an orbifold. The scan in T7 does not cover magnetized orbifolds.
- For Spin(32)/Z₂ the total first Chern number must be even on each two-cycle; this constrains m_n once m_u and m_d are chosen and was not used above.
- The noncommutative description of the diquark-like and one-label sectors (bimodules and their orientifold projection) was not worked out.
