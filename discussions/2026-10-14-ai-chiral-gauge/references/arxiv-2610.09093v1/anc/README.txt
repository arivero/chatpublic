Supplementary material for
A gauge-invariant measure for lattice chiral fermions
Nathaniel Craig

Three supplementary documents accompany the paper:

1. lean-verification-report.pdf
   Lean verification of the mathematical companion: the formalized results,
   source correspondence, verification procedures, and recorded outcomes.
2. mathematical-companion.pdf
   Detailed mathematical construction accompanying the formalization.
3. two-dimensional-supplement.pdf
   A two-dimensional chiral SU(5) measure, including its numerical calculations.

Associated repositories

Mathematical companion and Lean formalization:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23196062

Two-dimensional calculations:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23196101

The repositories provide the supplementary LaTeX sources and the corresponding
proof or calculation files. The historical scientific archives retain their
original filenames and source versions; each repository README explains them.

The two-dimensional supplement contains relative links to calculation evidence.
To use those links locally, download calculation_archive_2026_10_05.tar.gz from
its repository and extract it beside two-dimensional-supplement.pdf. This
creates calculation_archive_2026_10_05/, the directory used by the links.
These relative links do not resolve in the standalone arXiv-hosted PDF.
