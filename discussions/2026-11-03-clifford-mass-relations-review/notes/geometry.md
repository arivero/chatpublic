# What the geometric drawing does and does not show

The manuscript starts with the round spin-frame manifold `SU(2)≅S³`, which is a **three-dimensional manifold**, not the surface of an ordinary 3D ball. A sphere shown on paper is necessarily a *schematic representation* of S³; the true round S³ embeds in four Euclidean dimensions. Its intrinsic radius is prescribed as the **classical electron radius** `r_e=alpha*hbar/(m_e c)`, approximately 1/137 of the reduced electron Compton wavelength. Neither is an experimentally determined radius of a finite-size electron.

The lowest absolute Dirac eigenvalues on S³ are `±3/(2r)` and each eigenspace has complex dimension 2. Both signs give a four-dimensional complex space `S`; taking matrices on this space produces `W=End(S)` of complex dimension 16.

The author then picks two orthogonal directions in W: the scalar identity I and the Dirac sign / Clifford pseudoscalar Sigma. This yields a **prescribed** `1+14+1` Hilbert-space decomposition (scalar; 14-dimensional complement; pseudoscalar). These are dimensions of an auxiliary *operator space*, not dimensions of geometric pieces of the sphere.

The operator `C(X)=tr(Sigma X)I` has `C†C=16P_tau`; the response `A=(I-P_e)+C†C` has eigenvalues **0, 1, 17** on the three blocks. The weight 17 is **1+16**, not a new sphere harmonic or visually identifiable curvature. A different trace normalization would change it.

There are other decompositions under less restrictive symmetries. In particular the Spin(4) rotational commutant has dimension ten; the three-block physical reduction arises because the manuscript **postulates the full sector stabilizer** U(1)×U(14)×U(1).

For its proton extension, the author also uses the reduced Compton length `ell_e=hbar/(m_e c)` and takes the **difference** of the same lowest Dirac energy between radii ell_e and r_e. This relative energy is a separate baryonic prescription, not a universal geometric necessity.

The illustrative PNG files in `../figures/` should be treated as conceptual infographics, not literal mathematical embeddings or scale drawings. In particular, any depicted relative sizes of r_e and ell_e must not be interpreted literally; physically r_e is smaller by a factor alpha.
